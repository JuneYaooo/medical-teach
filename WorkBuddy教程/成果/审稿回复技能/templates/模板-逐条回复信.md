# 模板：逐条回复信（阶段 C 输出，`R{n}-逐条回复信.md`）

> 铁律：每条三段缺一不算写完；状态词只用 PROPOSED / BLOCKED / NO CHANGE / AWAITING STANCE（APPLIED 需稿件在本地且逐句核对）；同一封信不混语种。

```markdown
# R{n} 逐条回复信（Proposed Response Letter）

> **Note to the editor on locations.**（无固定分页时必写）
> The manuscript is not paginated in its current version; all locations are given by
> section and paragraph. No page or line numbers are invented.
>
> **Status vocabulary.** PROPOSED = proposed wording awaiting the author's merge;
> NO CHANGE = explanation only. No statement claims a change has already been made.

**Stance summary**

| Comment | Stance | Manuscript text proposed | Status |
|---|---|---|---|

---

### Comment n

> Reviewer comment:（照抄原文，不改写；中文意见可附英文译写并注明原文）

**Stance:** ____. **Status:** ____

**Proposed response.** …

*（一条两要求时拆两段小标题，如 *Rationale.* / *Citations and hard endpoints.*）*

**Proposed change.** 位置（Section x, paragraph y）｜拟稿正文｜Status: ____

---

（BLOCKED 条只写三行：缺口 / 两条出路 / 待作者选择，无任何正文。）

*End of letter. All manuscript text: PROPOSED (newly drafted; original manuscript not
provided). No statement above claims that a change has been applied.*
```

**交付包必须区分两类记录**：新拟稿（PROPOSED）与已合并修改（APPLIED，仅当稿件在本地、
已落稿且逐句核对，升级须在交叉核对表写明合并版本与核对日期）。既有修改记录只能引用，不能改写。
