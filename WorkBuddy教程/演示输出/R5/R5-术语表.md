## 6. 需要你择一的条目 ⚠️

| # | 分歧点 | 选项 A | 选项 B | 建议 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 效应量名称 | adjusted mean difference (aMD) | mean difference (MD) | **A**（首次全称，后文 MD） |
| 2 | 对照组称呼 | control group | comparator group | **A**（SAP 用"对照组"） |
| 3 | 未纳入者称呼 | not included in the primary analysis | excluded from the analysis | **A**（"excluded" 易与排除标准混淆） |
| 4 | 结局测量场景 | blood pressure at the 6-month visit | 6-month home blood pressure | **A**（数据字典 `sbp_6m` 为访视测量，无"家庭"属性） |
| 5 | 假设检验描述 | two-sided α = 0.05 | two-tailed p < 0.05 | **A** |

> 第 4 条尤其重要：把 `sbp_6m` 说成"家庭自测血压"会给审稿人送上新的把柄（干预是家庭监测，结局却是访视测量），也会让 C1 的硬终点问题变得更糟。

## 7. 禁用词清单（出现即改）

| 禁用 | 原因 | 替换 |
|---|---|---|
| demonstrate / prove / confirm（用于本研究结果） | 合成数据不能证因果 | was associated with / was lower in |
| efficacy / effective | 属临床有效性表述 | the observed difference in this simulated dataset |
| significant benefit | benefit 越界 | a statistically significant difference |
| trend towards significance | 统计误用 | 直接报点估计与 CI |
| reduced cardiovascular events | 未评估硬终点 | did not assess cardiovascular events |
| P = 0 | 不可能为 0 | P < 0.001（阈值） |
| exact P = <0.001 | 阈值不是精确值 | P < 0.001（正文）／exact P in Table 2 |
| page X, line Y | 稿件未分页 | Section, paragraph N |
| ITT analysis（单独使用） | 实为完整病例 | complete-case analysis; not a complete ITT analysis |
