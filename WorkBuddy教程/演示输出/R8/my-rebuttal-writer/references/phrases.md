# phrases.md — 句式库与禁用词

配套 `my-rebuttal-writer/SKILL.md`。**所有英文句子均为拟稿**，用前确认该条状态不是
`BLOCKED`。⛔ 为禁止写法，✅ 为可用写法。

---

## 1. 三句固定声明（放在信首，按实际情况增减）

**① 未分页声明（稿件无固定分页时必写）**

> The manuscript is not paginated in its current version, so all locations are given
> by section and paragraph rather than by page and line.

**② 稿件未落稿声明（稿件本体不在手时必写）**

> Locations below refer to the section and paragraph structure of the current manuscript.
> The changes described are proposed wording prepared for insertion; they have not been
> checked against a typeset version.

**③ 数据性质声明（合成/教学数据、非随机化时必写）**

> The dataset is simulated for teaching purposes; group assignment was generated by
> alternating participant identifiers rather than by randomisation. Observed differences
> are therefore described as differences in this dataset, not as evidence of clinical effect.

## 2. 接受

```text
We agree with the reviewer and have made this explicit in the manuscript.
```
（稿件在本地且已落稿时才可用 "have made"；否则改 §7 写法。）

更稳的写法，不依赖落稿状态：

```text
We agree with the reviewer. The proposed revision makes this explicit in the Methods.
```

**不要**用"全面接受"式表态开头——它逼你在后面每一处都兑现：

⛔ We fully agree with all of the reviewer's comments and have revised the manuscript accordingly.
✅ We agree with this comment. Our response to each point follows.

---

## 3. 部分接受

顺序固定：**已做的 → 未做的 → 为什么不能更强 → 缺口怎么标**。

```text
We share the reviewer's concern. We can address part of it directly, and we would like to
be explicit about the part we cannot.

*What we can state.* No imputation of any kind, including mean imputation, was applied;
the primary analysis is a complete-case analysis and is described as such throughout.

*What we cannot state.* The missing-data mechanism cannot be determined from the comparisons
available to us. The observed differences between groups do not establish that data are
missing at random, nor that they are not.

*What remains outstanding.* Sensitivity analyses for missing outcome data — for example
multiple imputation and worst-case scenarios — have not been performed. We do not report
any such analysis, and we state this in the Limitations rather than leaving the reader to
assume one was done.
```

⛔ …and we will perform multiple imputation in the revision.
（承诺新实验，红线 R-4）

## 4. 不接受实质要求

四段结构：**理解诉求 → 逐条理由（可核对）→ 替代方案 → 留门**。

```text
We thank the reviewer for this suggestion, which we understand as a wish to know whether
the effect is consistent across patient subgroups. We have not added the subgroup analyses,
and we would like to explain why.

First, the analysis plan prespecified no subgroup analyses and stated that no post hoc
forest plot would be produced.

Second, with 268 participants in the primary analysis, dividing the sample across five
subgroups would leave approximately 27 participants per group; a within-subgroup comparison
would require approximately 72 per group for 80% power at a two-sided alpha of 0.05.

Third, highlighting the subgroup with the smallest P value among five unadjusted comparisons
would constitute selective reporting. Even under no effect, the probability of at least one
P < 0.05 among five independent tests is approximately 23%.

Fourth, effect modification is properly examined by a treatment-by-subgroup interaction test,
reported for all prespecified subgroups including negative findings.

Should the editor consider a stratified presentation essential, we would be willing to
provide descriptive summaries for all prespecified baseline variables, without significance
testing, without ranking by P value, and explicitly labelled as exploratory.
```

要点：理由里出现的每个数字（27、72、23%）必须能当场复算；**推算值不得混进结果数字表**。

## 5. 引文不支持作者主张

答案只有一个方向：**降结论强度**，不是"再找几篇"。

```text
We agree with the reviewer's implicit concern, and we have concluded that the cited
references do not support a benefit on hard endpoints. The sources we cite describe home
measurement technique and its use for blood pressure control, and the trial we cite
reported office systolic blood pressure as its primary outcome rather than cardiovascular
events.
```

⛔ The cited references are somewhat dated; we have added newer references supporting the
hard-endpoint benefit.

## 6. 页码不可用

```text
The manuscript is not paginated in its current version, so page and line numbers cannot be
given. All locations are specified by section and paragraph, for example "Methods,
Statistical analysis, paragraph 3". We have not supplied page numbers, because any number
we could give would not correspond to the typeset article.
```

⛔ Methods, page 6, lines 12–18.（编的）
⛔ Approximately page 6.（"approximately" 也是编）
✅ Methods, Statistical analysis, paragraph 3.

**作者坚持要页码时**：说明稿件未分页、给出章节段落、并提示"校样阶段可补"——但**不承诺**
（"will supply at proof stage" 属期约，需作者签字同意）。

## 7. 未落稿时：Proposed

稿件本体不在工作区（或尚未插入）时，标题与动词都要改：

| ⛔ | ✅ |
|---|---|
| We have added a sentence to the Methods. | **Proposed change.** Methods, Statistical analysis, paragraph 2 — sentence added: "…" |
| We have removed all efficacy wording. | **Proposed change.** Abstract, last sentence — efficacy wording replaced with: "…" |
| We have revised the Discussion accordingly. | **Proposed change.** Discussion, Limitations, paragraph 1 — proposed replacement: "…" |
| We have checked every location cited in this letter. | Every location cited in this letter is specified by section and paragraph. |

## 8. 缺证据：只列缺口

BLOCKED 条的全部正文，就这三行：

```text
**Proposed response.** [BLOCKED — 该条暂不撰拟]

- 缺口：<需要的证据> 不在当前底稿中（底稿仅有：<已列文件>）。
- 出路一：提供 <具体输出文件/表>，我据实写。
- 出路二：改为如实陈述未做（"was not performed"），且不承诺补做。
- 待作者选择。
```

⛔ We will provide these analyses upon request.
⛔ These analyses are underway and will be included.
✅ These analyses have not been performed; we do not report them and make no claim that
they were done.

## 9. 缺失数据三档表述

| 情形 | 可用 | 禁用 |
|---|---|---|
| 机制未判定 | The mechanism cannot be determined from these comparisons; the complete-case analysis may be biased, and the direction of any such bias is unknown. | "data were MAR" / "missingness was not at random" |
| 组间缺失比例不同 | 21/150 (14.0%) vs 10/150 (6.7%), reported descriptively; no statistical test was performed. | "significantly higher"（未做检验） |
| 稳健标准误 | HC3 standard errors address the variance specification only. | "a sensitivity analysis for missing data" |

⛔ Missingness was related to group allocation, therefore the data are not MAR.
（两组缺失比例不同**不能**推出机制；MAR 允许缺失依赖已观测变量）

## 10. 禁用词 → 替换

| 禁用 | 原因 | 替换 |
|---|---|---|
| demonstrate / prove | 合成数据不能"证明" | was observed；in this dataset |
| effective / efficacy | 越界为临床结论 | difference in blood pressure；no clinical inference |
| significant（未做检验时） | 统计词滥用 | higher / lower（描述） |
| trend towards significance | 无此概念 | not statistically tested |
| P = 0 | 不可能 | P < 0.001 |
| will be added / performed | 承诺新实验 | 不写；或 have not been performed |
| is being prepared | 期约 | 不写；或 not yet available |
| we have checked / verified | 未落稿即不实 | is specified by section and paragraph |
| 全面接受 / 已全部修改 | 无法兑现 | 逐条说明 |

## 11. 数字与精度

- 点估计两位小数；CI 用 `−5.60 (95% CI −7.67 to −3.52)`，全文连接词统一（`to` 或 `–`，不混）。
- 标准误与源表同精度（源表三位就写三位，别在正文悄悄变两位）。
- 同一指标全文一个精度。Table 里 0.7 (0.2)，正文就不要写 0.69。
- 百分数保留一位；`21/150` 与 `14.0%` 同时给出，读者可复算。
- **每个数字后面在心里问一句：出处是哪个文件的哪一行。** 答不上来就不写。

## 12. 中文速查

| 场景 | 中文拟稿 |
|---|---|
| 接受 | 我们同意审稿人的意见。拟在 Methods 第 2 段补入如下表述：…… |
| 部分接受 | 我们能直接回应其中一部分，另一部分需要明确说明无法做到。 |
| 不接受 | 我们理解审稿人关注的是效应在亚组间是否一致。我们未新增该分析，理由如下四点。 |
| 缺口 | 该分析目前未做，回复中不报告，也不称其已完成。 |
| 位置 | 位置按章节与段落标注（稿件当前版本未分页）。 |
